Testing is the last gate before launch

Every satellite has to pass vibration and thermal vacuum testing before it flies. For small teams, that critical step still runs on inboxes and guesswork.

And demand is rising: a record 4,517 satellites reached orbit in 2025.

Source: Starlust, 2025 launch review (2026).

Emails

Quotes, slots and test specs bounce between engineers and labs in long email threads.

Spreadsheets

Test plans, levels and results sit in spreadsheets that drift out of date and are hard to audit.

Separate lab calendars

Every lab keeps its own calendar. Finding a free shaker or chamber means chasing each one.

Vibration testing
Shaking the satellite to check it survives the loads of launch.
Thermal vacuum
Running it in a vacuum chamber through the temperature swings of space.
Qualification
Proving, with evidence, that the design meets its test requirements.

How it works

In development

One platform takes your test campaign from first plan to final evidence, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Plan

    Set out your campaign in one shared plan: test levels, sequence and the qualification requirements you need to meet.

  2. 02

    Book

    See availability across UK test labs and book a slot in one place. No more chasing calendars.

  3. 03 Our differentiator

    Analyse

    Our AI watches test data live and flags problems while the test is still running, not after it ends. So you can act before the run is over.

  4. 04

    Prove

    Turn results into a clear, traceable record of what was tested and how it performed, ready to share.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about how Groundproof works.

Does the AI control the test?

No. It only advises. The lab's own safety limits stay in charge of the test.

Who owns the test data?

You do. Customers own and control their data, and decide what is shared.

Is Groundproof live yet?

Not yet. We're building the prototype now and looking for a test lab to pilot with.

Do I need to buy any equipment?

No. Groundproof is software. Labs keep their own equipment.

We're looking for a test lab to pilot with

If you run vibration or thermal vacuum testing in the UK, we'd love to talk. Satellite teams who want early access are welcome too.
